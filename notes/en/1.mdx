## Why this exists

This is not the blog. The blog is where tutorials live, with a beginning, a middle and an end.

A note is the draft. An idea I'm testing, a UI problem I want to think through out loud, a `snippet` that saved my day. No intro, no conclusion, no SEO.

## How it works

Every note gets a number. No title, no tags, no date up front. You open it, read it, close it.

If one of them grows into a real post, it moves to the blog.
